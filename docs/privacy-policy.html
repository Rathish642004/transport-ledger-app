Transport Ledger — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026

Transport Ledger is a personal, single-user Android app for recording transport orders, customers, companies, bank accounts, expenses, and payments. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

Data storage

All ledger data you enter (orders, customers, companies, banks, expenses, payments) is stored locally on your device in a local database. This app has no backend server, and the developer does not collect, view, or have access to any of this data.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you choose to connect Google Drive from the Settings screen, the app requests the drive.appdata Google Drive scope, which is the most restrictive Drive scope Google offers. Under this scope, the app can only read and write a single backup file it creates itself, in your Google Drive account's hidden, application-specific data folder. This means:

  • The backup file does not appear in your visible "My Drive" file list.
  • No other files in your Google Drive can be read, listed, or modified by this app.
  • The backup file is used only to restore your ledger data if you reinstall the app or switch devices.

You can disconnect Google Drive at any time from Settings, which revokes the app's access to your Google account. Uninstalling the app has the same effect on the device, and you can also revoke access directly at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

What this app does not do

  • No advertising or ad networks.
  • No analytics or usage tracking.
  • No third-party data sharing or selling of any kind.
  • No account system — sign-in is only used to authorize the optional Drive backup described above.

Permissions used

The app requests Android's INTERNET permission, used solely for the optional Google Drive backup/restore calls described above. Sharing a PDF bill or ledger export, or tapping a phone number to call a contact, hands off to apps you choose on your device (e.g. your dialer or a messaging app) — the app itself does not collect data through these actions.

Children's privacy

This app is a business tool and is not directed at children.
